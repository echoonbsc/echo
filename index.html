Listener questions

Is the free share really free?

Yes — claimFree() costs only network gas. One per wallet, 600 in total, enforced by the contract itself.

Where does the money go?

100% of every buy() payment is forwarded to the treasury inside the same transaction. The contract keeps nothing and has no withdrawal function.

What happens to unsold shares?

They are burned at sign-off. After 23 Nov 23:30 WIB anyone can call finalize(), which destroys every ECHO still held by the contract.

Can the station change the program?

No. The price, caps, window and treasury are constants in a verified, immutable contract. There is no owner who could retune them.
